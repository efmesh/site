# :material-radio-handheld: Recommended Hardware for the Forest

You only need **one radio** to get on the mesh. Charge it, pair it with your phone, and you're set.

### SenseCAP MeshTracker X1

<span class="ef-pick__price">~$50</span>

Card-sized, clips to your pack, works out of the box. There's no screen: you read and send messages in the Meshtastic app on your phone.

- **Charges with a normal USB-C cable**, so any phone charger or battery pack works
- Waterproof (IP66), up to ~5 days of battery
- Built-in GPS so your squad can see where you are

## :material-cart-outline: Buying Tips

- **Order early.** Stock sells out and overseas shipping can take 2–4 weeks. You'll also want time to set it up and test it before Forest.
- **US sellers ship faster.** Rokland and Atlavox keep stock in the US. Overseas stores can be cheaper, but they're slower and can add customs fees.
- **On Amazon, check the seller.** Make sure it's the exact model above, sold by a real store.
